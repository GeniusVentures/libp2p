<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| Untrusted inbound TCP accept -> TcpListener | Any remote peer can attempt to connect; interceptAccept is the earliest possible policy checkpoint, before any bytes beyond the TCP handshake are exchanged |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| T-01-10 | Denial of Service | TcpListener::doAccept() rejection path | high | mitigate | GATE-05: every rejected accept closes the TcpConnection via its hardened close() (guarded by !isClosed()), preventing fd leaks under repeated rejected-then-retried inbound connections |
| T-01-11 | Information Disclosure | Accept-then-secure ordering | high | mitigate | UpgraderSession/secureInbound() is only ever constructed after interceptAccept accepts, inside the same deferred callback — a rejected accept never exchanges a single security-handshake byte (roadmap criterion 3) |
| T-01-12 | Tampering (reentrancy) | async_accept completion handler | medium | mitigate | The interceptAccept hook call itself is moved into a scheduler_->schedule(...)-deferred callback rather than invoked inline in the asio completion handler, per the resolved design and this codebase's documented TCP-transport reentrancy history (.planning/codebase/CONCERNS.md) |
| T-01-13 | Denial of Service | Accept-loop stalling | medium | mitigate | self->doAccept() is called synchronously immediately after scheduling the deferred gater check, so a single pending/slow gater decision never blocks new inbound accepts |
</threat_model>
